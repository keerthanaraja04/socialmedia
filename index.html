💡 What is a Carousel Deck?
A carousel (or swipe deck) is a multi-slide visual presentation (1080x1350 portrait) made for LinkedIn & Instagram. Instead of 1 static post, users swipe horizontally through 3 cards:
• Slide 1 (Hook): Bold, scroll-stopping headline with category pill badge.
• Slide 2 (Value Framework): Actionable takeaway bullet points & tips.
• Slide 3 (CTA): Call-to-action ("Follow @IncuBrix", "Link in bio").
